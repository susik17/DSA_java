In competitive programming and technical interviews, problem constraints are usually given as powers of 10 (e.g., \(N \le 10^5\)).
An environment like LeetCode or Codeforces allows about \(10^{8}\) operations per second. 
To avoid a TLE (Time Limit Exceeded) error, your Big O Time Complexity (\(TC\)) must match the constraints.
